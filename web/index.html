SegelQ

Quantum-sealed prediction receipts on QoreChain. Seal a prediction today; prove later — word for word — that you called it, with a receipt anchored to a hybrid post-quantum signature (secp256k1 + ML-DSA-87, NIST FIPS 204) verified by the chain itself.

Testnet · qorechain-diana EVM chain ID 9800 seals on chain: …

1 · Hash a prediction

Type a short prediction. Only its SHA-256 hash ever goes on chain — the text stays with you until you choose to reveal it. Sealing is done from the project CLI (pnpm seal) or a QoreX window session; this page verifies the receipts.

2 · Verify a hybrid-signed receipt

Paste the hash of the hybrid-signed window-open transaction. SegelQ reads it back from the public QoreChain REST endpoint and shows both signatures and the validators' pqc_hybrid_verify verdict.

3 · Read a seal

Look up a seal stored in the SegelQ contract by its id.
